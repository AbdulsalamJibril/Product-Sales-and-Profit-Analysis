# Product-Sales-and-Profit-Analysis

## Table of content



### Project overview

This project analyzes product sales and profitability using an interactive dashboard. The goal is to provide a clear view of revenue, cost, and profit performance across products, time periods, locations, sales teams, and sales channels.

The dashboard summarizes key financial metrics and highlights areas that contribute most to overall profit. By visualizing trends and performance differences, the project helps stakeholders quickly understand business performance and identify key profit drivers.

### Data source

Sales Data: The primary dataset used for this analysis is the "sales_data.csv" file,containing detailed information about each product sales and profit made by the company.

### Tools

Power BI,I used it to extract the data, clean the data, analyze sales and profit, and build the dashboard. It also made it easy to create visuals and interact with the data using filters.

### Data Cleaning/Preparation

I cleaned and prepared the data in Power BI so it could be used confidently for analysis. I checked for missing values, fixed incorrect data types, and removed columns that weren’t useful. I also made a few simple adjustments to keep everything consistent and added calculated fields to better understand sales and profit.

### Exploratory Data Analysis

At this stage, the data was explored to see what was really going on.

1- Reviewed overall sales, cost, and profit to understand general performance.


2- Compared product categories to see which products performed best.


3- Checked monthly trends to observe changes in sales and profit over time.


4- Analyzed performance by state to identify top and low-performing locations.


5- Looked at sales team contributions to see differences in individual performance.


6- Compared sales channels to understand where profit was coming from.

### Analysis/Findings

A quick look at the dashboard shows where profit is coming from and how performance differs across the business.


1- Overall performance is strong with a healthy profit margin.


2- Clocks and Vases generate the highest profit.


3- Profit improves over time, especially in later months.


4- California leads in both revenue and profit.

5- A few sales reps contribute a large share of total profit.

### Data visulaization

This dashboard provides a clear view of sales and profit performance. Key metrics at the top show total revenue, cost, profit, and profit margin. Below this, visuals break down profit by product, month, state, sales team, and sales channel, making it easy to see where results are strongest. Filters for month and product category allow users to quickly adjust the view and focus on specific areas of interest.

### Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business can improve results by focusing more on high-performing products like Clocks and Vases, building on the success seen in strong markets such as California, and using the practices of top sales reps as a guide for the wider team. It is also important to continue supporting both Online and In-store channels, since they contribute almost equally to overall profit.
